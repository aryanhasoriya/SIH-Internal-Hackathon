ABOUT THE SELECTION ROUND

THIS IS YOUR
SELECTION ROUND.

The College Level Internal Hackathon is the central internal selection round for Smart India Hackathon 2026. Teams will present their problem statement, idea and PPT, followed by a demonstration of their working or functional prototype.

SELECTION GOAL
TOP 50 TEAMS

The best 50 performing teams will be shortlisted based on their performance in this Internal Hackathon for participation in Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026.

SIH 2026 CREATIVE IDENTITY BUILD & DEMO

Central Selection Round

Official internal institutional gateway to shortlist teams representing the college at SIH 2026.

PPT & Working Prototype

Teams must articulate their problem statement clearly, show their PPT, and demo a functional prototype.

Objective Judging Panel

Evaluated by external industry & academic experts with rigorous criteria and transparent scoring.

THE CHALLENGE

WHAT YOU NEED TO DO

A 4-step pathway from problem statement to securing your spot among the Top 50.

STEP 01

KNOW YOUR PROBLEM

Present your chosen problem statement with sharp clarity, deep context, and target user focus.

STEP 02

PERFECT YOUR PITCH

Deliver a compelling presentation covering solution architecture, feasibility, and impact.

STEP 03

DEMONSTRATE PROTOTYPE

Showcase a real working prototype with core functionality and real-time execution.

STEP 04

IMPRESS THE JUDGES

Answer technical cross-examination with confidence and secure your spot in the Top 50.

JUDGING CRITERIA & FLOW

EXTERNAL JUDGES.
REAL EVALUATION.

Evaluation will be conducted by external judges to ensure an objective, rigorous, and transparent selection process.

01

Problem Understanding

Evaluation of problem statement depth, user research, and domain context.

02

Idea & PPT Quality

Innovation, uniqueness of approach, feasibility, and presentation structure.

03

Prototype Demo

Live working demonstration showing technical implementation and functionality.

04

External Judging

Rigorous questioning, viability review, and objective scoring by external experts.

05

Top 50 Shortlisting

Final selection of the best 50 performing teams for institutional nomination to SIH 2026.

COMPLIANCE NOTICE STRICT ENFORCEMENT

NO AI. NO PLAGIARISM.
